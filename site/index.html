What areas do you service?

We cover Brisbane and the Redlands. If you're nearby and not sure, just call.

Do you do gas work as well as plumbing?

Yes. We're licensed for both plumbing and gas fitting, so we can handle gas cooktops, heaters, hot water, gas lines and leak checks as well as all your plumbing.

Will I know the price before you start?

Yes. We'll inspect the problem, explain what's needed and give you a clear price. Nothing starts until you're happy with it.

Can you help with an emergency?

Call us straight away on 0467 505 883. For a burst pipe, turn off your water at the meter. If you smell gas, turn off the gas at the meter, open windows and don't use switches or flames, then call us or the gas emergency line.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes. As well as homes, we look after shops, offices, rentals and other commercial properties across Brisbane and the Redlands.

How do I get a quote?

Call or text 0467 505 883, or fill in the callback form at the top of this page. Texting a photo of the problem often helps us give you a faster idea of cost.
